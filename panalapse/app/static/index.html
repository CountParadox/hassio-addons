Home Assistant

  

    

      
MQTT 

      
    

    
Each timelapse and camera appears in Home Assistant as a device, with its status, last and next capture, frame count, a “problem” sensor, the latest image, an on/off switch and a “Capture now” button.

    

      
      
      
      
      
      
      
    

    
    
Failures also fire the events panalapse_capture_failed and panalapse_capture_recovered, so an automation can send them to your phone.
